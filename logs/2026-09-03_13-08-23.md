| **Best For** | High-throughput, cost-sensitive tasks | Document workflows, efficiency-first apps | Agentic coding, multi-step reasoning, complex enterprise tasks |

## ⚠️ Migration Notes

- **3.6 → 3.7:** Pure quality upgrade at half the launch price.
- **3.7 → 3.8:** Same price and speed envelope, but ~5–8% benchmark gains. Google explicitly recommends **staying on 3.7 Flash** if compute efficiency is your priority, because 3.8 burns more tokens per task.
- **No reason to start new projects on 3.7 Flash** — Google recommends 3.8 Flash as the default for new builds.

---

**Bottom line:** 3.6 Flash was an efficiency upgrade, 3.7 Flash was an algorithmic refinement, and 3.8 Flash is the same architecture re-tuned for harder reasoning at the cost of more tokens per call. The price has stayed flat at $0.75/$3.75 throughout — but watch the **Jan 1, 2027 doubling** if you're budgeting at scale.
